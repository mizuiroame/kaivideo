# Controls

[Back to the README](../README.md)

Every control works with the buttons and the touch screen. The line at the
bottom of the screen always says which buttons do what, as in KaiPlayer and
KaiRadio.

## In the library

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **ZL** / **ZR** (or **L** / **R**) | The tab before or after: Home, Films, Series (or your server's libraries), Search, Files |
| **↑** **↓** **◀** **▶**, or either stick | Move (a card or a row at a time) |
| **A** | Play the film or episode, or open the series |
| **B** | Back from a series (in Files: the folder above) |
| **X** | Go through the sources: the SD card, the network folder and each server (with none other set up: Settings, to add one) |
| **Y** | Search |
| **−** | Settings |
| **L** | On a server's film or episode (its panel) or series' page: download it, or the season |
| **+** | The menu: Downloads, Update the library, Settings, Exit (**+** again asks to exit, and **+** once more exits) |

- **Live TV**: **↑** / **↓** change channel, **◀** / **▶** move through the
  guide (a day ahead) and **A** watches the channel. On a Jellyfin server,
  **R** records the program (or its series) and **L** lists the recordings
  ([more](media-servers.md#recording-jellyfins-dvr)).
- **A series**: **◀** / **▶** change the season, and **X** marks the episode
  watched or not (or tap its circle on the right).
- **Files**: **◀** / **▶** on the D-pad move a page, and the path at the top
  goes to the folder above.
- **Menus and panels** (the + menu, Settings, the video's lists, the streams to
  choose from, IPTV groups): the right stick moves like the D-pad.
- **Touch**: tap a tab, a card or a row to open it, and drag to scroll. Lists
  follow your finger and glide on when you let go, as on a phone; a tap stops
  them. The **☰** before the name opens the menu.

## While a video plays

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **A** | Pause or play |
| **◀** / **▶** | Back or forward 10 seconds |
| **L** / **R** | Back or forward 1 minute (on Live TV: the channel before or after) |
| **ZL** | Start over |
| **ZR** | **Skip intro** (or recap, or credits) when its button shows; in an episode's last 30 seconds, play the next one now; on a Jellyfin channel, record what's on |
| **↑** | The [Playback list](playback.md#the-playback-list) |
| **↓** | Statistics |
| **X** | The list of sound tracks |
| **Y** | The list of subtitles |
| **−** | Hide the bar (**−** again, or a tap, shows it) |
| **B** | Back to the library (where you were is remembered) |
| **+** | The menu, over the video: Back to the library, then the same items as on the library (Downloads, Update the library and Settings stop the video first) |

- **Lists**: **↑** / **↓** move, **A** picks and **B** closes. The subtitles
  list stays open after you pick, for the options that apply to them (**◀** /
  **▶**).
- **The Playback list**: **A** goes to a chapter, and **◀** / **▶** change
  the options (or tap the left or right half of their row). On **A-B loop**,
  each press sets A where the video is, then B, then turns it off; at B the
  video goes back to A.
- **The bar**: any button or tap shows it for a few seconds. With it showing:
  - tap the picture or the play button to pause or play (a paused video shows
    a big play symbol);
  - tap or drag the progress bar to go there;
  - **Playback**, **Audio** or **CC** open their lists;
  - the **×** at the top left goes back.
